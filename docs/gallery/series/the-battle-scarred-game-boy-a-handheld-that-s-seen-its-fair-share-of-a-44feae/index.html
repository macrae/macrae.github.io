Battle-scarred Gameboy (2)

2 takes · 2023-01-07 · all images

The Battle-Scarred Game Boy - a handheld that's seen its fair share of action. Its surface is dented and battered, with scraps of metal and shards of glass held together by a network of wires. It's powered by the pure, unbridled rage of its former owners, and it's said to contain a never-ending supply of intense, metal game worlds within its screen.
