Gameboy by REI

The Stone Game Boy - a handheld carved from a single block of granite. Its buttons are formed from polished gemstones, and its screen is made of a sheet of shimmering obsidian. It's said to contain the strength and endurance of the earth within its pixels, and it's a must-have for any eco-punk gamer.
